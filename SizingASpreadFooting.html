Step 1: Calculate Original Footing Area

Find the area of the existing footing before we check the load requirements.

Step 2: Net Allowable Pressure (q-net)

q-net = q-a - (Thickness * 150)

Step 3: Minimum Required Area

Area Required = Column Load / q-net

Step 4: Engineering Recommendation

How to compare: Look at your Original Area (Step 1) and your Minimum Required Area (Step 3).

If Original Area ≥ Required Area, the footing is Safe. If Original Area < Required Area, the footing is Unsafe and must be redesigned.
